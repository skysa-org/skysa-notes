Secrets are now part of the source's vault. The menu item on the gear and a source's `⋯` says "Vault…", or "Set up vault…" until the source has one, and the dialog it opens is called "Vault". Setting up, unlocking, the recovery key's printed page ("Vault recovery key for …") and a new device-lock passkey ("Unlocks the vault on this device: …") say the vault too; a passkey made before keeps its old name and still works. A secret in a note is still a secret, and nothing in anyone's notes or keys changes.
