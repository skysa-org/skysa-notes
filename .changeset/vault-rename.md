The Secrets feature is now called Vault. The menu item on the gear and a source's `⋯` says "Vault…", or "Set up vault…" until the source has one, the dialog it opens is called "Vault", and a note's warning opens it with "Set up vault" or "Open Vault". Setting up, unlocking, the recovery key's printed page ("Vault recovery key for …") and a new device-lock passkey ("Unlocks the vault on this device: …") say the vault too; a passkey made before keeps its old name and still works. A secret in a note is still a secret, and nothing in anyone's notes or keys changes.
