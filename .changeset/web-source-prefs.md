A source connected again under a new id brings back what this device remembered of it: its pinned notes and notebooks, which notebooks were open, the note open last in each, whether the scratchpad and passwords were shown, and its notes among the recent ones. These are merged with whatever the new connection already remembered. A source this device forgets now forgets them too, and so does the device's own pile once its notes are in a source.
