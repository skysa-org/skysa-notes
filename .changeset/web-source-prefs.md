Pins now survive disconnecting and connecting again. When a source's notes leave the device, this device keeps what it remembered about that account: pinned notes and notebooks, which notebooks were open, the note open last in each, and whether the scratchpad and passwords were shown. All of it comes back when the account is connected again. It also goes with notes moved into another source, and with the device's own notes when they go into the first source you connect.
