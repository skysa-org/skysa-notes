Secrets: a recovery key is copied in its groups, as it is shown, and is typed into twelve numbered fields, one for each group. Each field goes on to the next as it fills, and a key pasted whole fills them all. The printed key has a page margin of its own. In the Secrets and unlock dialogs, each step's buttons and Cancel or Close are now in one row, as in the app's other dialogs.
