Every dialog is drawn one way, redrawn after trying them:

- A dialog you can leave has a ✕ in its corner, and a press on the page around it leaves it too, as Escape does. Once nothing is being asked, the ✕ is the only way out: there is no Close button in the row.
- The answer sits alone on the right of the button row; Cancel and anything else are on the left.
- A dialog is never taller than the window. Its title and buttons stay put while what is between them scrolls. On a phone, the field being typed in stays in sight above the keyboard.
- On a phone, every dialog takes the window's width, so a row of three buttons fits on one line.
- A notebook's Attached files scrolls as a whole rather than in a box of its own.
- A printed recovery key has a margin on every side again, rather than starting at the paper's left edge.
