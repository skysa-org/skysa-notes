The layout follows the width of the window. Below 1400px the outline starts
collapsed, with an Outline button in the note's header to open it. Below
1250px the notebook and note columns narrow with the window. In a window as
narrow as a phone, the source tabs become a dropdown, the notebooks and the
notes become dropdowns beside it, the search becomes an icon, and the
source dropdown holds the storage panel — what is syncing and the way to
disconnect — above the way to connect another account. The
formatting toolbar is hidden until the Format button shows it at the bottom of
the note. Pressing Escape on the `+` beside the tabs now closes its menu, and a
second press on the `+` closes it rather than reopening it.
