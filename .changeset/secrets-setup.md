"Set up secrets…", in a source's gear and its `⋯`, sets the source up for secrets: a new recovery key, shown once with Print and Copy and checked by typing two of its groups back, or a key you already have. Each device then unlocks with a pattern, a PIN or a password of its own, which can be changed later without the recovery key. A source already set up asks for its recovery key instead, and a key that would leave existing secrets locked says so first. Writing secrets into notes comes next.
