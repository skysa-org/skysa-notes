You can choose files to put in a note in three places: the paperclip on the toolbar, Image or File in the slash menu, and "Attach files" in the command palette. The palette command works in both editors. What you choose goes beside the note and takes the place of the selection, just as a pasted file does. On a phone, Image asks for photos. Toolbar buttons that are not toggles, such as indentation and clearing formatting, are no longer announced to screen readers as "not pressed".
