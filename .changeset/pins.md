Notebooks and notes can be pinned to the top of their list from the `⋯` on their row, or a right-click: "Pin to top", and "Unpin" once pinned. A pinned notebook goes to the top of the notebooks under its own parent, a pinned note to the top of its notebook's list, and a pinned row is tinted. Pins are kept on this device, per source, and follow a notebook that is renamed or moved.
