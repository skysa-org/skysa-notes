The app moves more smoothly, and all of a piece. Hover, selection and anything switched on fade in rather than blink. Menus open out of the button that opened them, upward where they rise from the foot of the sidebar or of a phone's note. Dialogs and the command palette rise in as the page behind them dims, and notices slide up from the bottom. A notebook's notebooks, new clipboard items and the find bar fade in. With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, nothing moves and only colours fade.
