Google Drive: a notebook moved, renamed or deleted on one device no longer stays behind as an empty notebook on your other devices, or comes back after a re-scan. Its old folder on Drive is marked as left instead of being kept as a notebook, and nothing in it is touched, so anything you put there yourself through Drive is safe. Making a notebook with that name again brings it back. Folders left behind before this update still show; remove them once from the app, or from drive.google.com.
