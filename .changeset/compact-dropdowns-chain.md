In a narrow window, choosing in one dropdown opens the next: a source chosen opens its notebooks, and a notebook its notes, so a phone goes from an account to a note in one pass. Going from one dropdown straight to another slides them along together — the one left goes off to the side and the next comes in from the other — in the order their names sit in the bar. A notebook with nothing in it still goes straight to the new note begun in it, and a user who has asked for reduced motion gets each panel at once.
