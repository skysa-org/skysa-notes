A device that unlocks secrets with its own lock (fingerprint, face or screen lock) asks for it straight away. Opening a secret while the device is locked shows the device's prompt with no Unlock dialog to tap through first, and the Secrets dialog's Unlock does the same. If the prompt is cancelled, the Unlock dialog appears with Try again as its answer, Cancel beside it, and "Use the recovery key" on a line of its own above them.
