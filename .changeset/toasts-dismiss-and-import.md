The notice after deleting a note now goes away as soon as you click or tap anywhere else, and closes with an × in its corner, so Undo is the one button on it. A long name with no spaces in it, such as a scratch note that is only a link, now wraps inside the notice instead of pushing its buttons out of the box. How an import went is now said in a notice over the page, like the others, instead of under the storage panel or in the sources menu.
