Passwords in the vault. Turn on "Passwords" in a source's Vault dialog, and a Passwords row appears under the notebooks on that device. It lists the source's passwords by label, with a filter and `+` for a new one. A password has a label, which is not encrypted, and a username, a password and notes, which are encrypted with the vault's key as they are typed. Copy puts the username or the password on the clipboard, Show reveals the password, and Edit and Delete work as they do for a note, with Undo after a delete.
