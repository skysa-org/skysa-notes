Secrets in the rich editor. A secret shows as a closed block that says "Secret" and offers Reveal, which unlocks the device if it is locked and opens the words in a panel under the block, with Copy, Cancel and Done. What you type there is encrypted into the note as you leave the panel or press Done, and only if it changed; Cancel puts the secret back as it was. "Secret" in the slash menu, and "Insert secret" in the palette, write a new one. When the app locks, every open secret closes, and changed words are saved first.
