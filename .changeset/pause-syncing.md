You can see what a sync is doing, and pause it. While the storage panel's status says it is syncing, tap the status to open a window showing what it is doing, how far it has got and the file it is on, with **Pause syncing**. Paused, nothing syncs on this device: your edits are kept here and sent once you choose **Resume syncing** or **Sync now**, re-scan, switch to another source and back, or open the app again. The status says "Paused" meanwhile.
