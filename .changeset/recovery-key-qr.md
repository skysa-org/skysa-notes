The recovery key now comes with a QR code, on screen and on the printed page. Wherever the key is asked for, Scan QR code reads it with the camera instead of typing it. The frames are read on the device, and the camera is let go of as soon as a key is read or the scan is cancelled. On a phone, the key's groups now sit three to a row, so none is cut off at the dialog's edge.
