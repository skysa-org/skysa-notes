Notes can be imported from a folder or from files (a `.zip`, such as the one "Download all notes" makes, or loose `.md` files), from the storage panel of the source showing. Notebooks and the files beside notes come too. Before anything is written, a question says what will come in and what stays out and why: notes that are not UTF-8 text, notes the app keeps hidden, and files over 25 MB. Nothing already in the source is changed: a taken name gets a number, and a file already there is not sent again. This is the way to bring notes into a Google Drive source, where files copied into the folder on the Drive website are invisible to the app; the Drive notice now points to it.
